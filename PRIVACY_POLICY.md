# Campmatch Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

Campmatch ("we", "us") respects student privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is processed, and how account controls work.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Credentials:** University email address (`.edu.gh`) and bcrypt-hashed password (or Google OAuth2 identifier).
- **Profile Details:** Name, age, academic level, programme, residence hall/hostel, gender, discovery preferences, bio, prompts, interests, and uploaded profile photos.
- **Automated Photo Screening Metrics:** Uploaded images are processed on our server to reject blank, solid-color, non-human, blurry, or explicit images. Technical check metadata (such as pass/reject reason) is logged for moderation integrity.
- **Activity & Communications:** Swipes, matches, chat messages, reactions, blocks, and safety reports.
- **Location:** Optional coordinates used only to compute approximate kilometer distance between profiles. Exact coordinates are never shared with other users.
- **Payments:** Subscription transaction references, amounts, and status processed through Paystack. We do not store card or mobile-money PIN credentials.

## 2. How We Use Information
- Authenticate university students and maintain campus-only access.
- Rank and display compatible profiles according to your selected mode (`Date`, `BFF`, `Study`) and discovery filters.
- Deliver real-time match notifications, typing indicators, and private messages.
- Enforce daily usage quotas, prevent abuse, investigate safety reports, and maintain platform security.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only:
- With other authenticated students to display your public profile card and match messages.
- With payment providers (such as Paystack) when you initiate a Plus subscription.
- When required by applicable law or necessary to protect student safety.

## 4. Account Pausing & Deletion
- **Snooze Mode:** You may hide your profile from discovery decks at any time from Settings while keeping existing conversations.
- **Account Deletion:** When you delete your account in Settings, your profile, photos, and matches are immediately removed from public visibility and active sessions are closed. Historical database records are retained in restricted administrative storage for safety, fraud prevention, and audit compliance.

## 5. Security
Passwords are stored using salted bcrypt hashes, session tokens are versioned for immediate revocation, payment webhooks are verified via HMAC-SHA512 signatures, and API endpoints enforce rate limiting and security headers.

## 6. Eligibility
Campmatch is strictly for university students aged 18 and older.

## 7. Contact
For privacy inquiries: `admin@st.umat.edu.gh`
