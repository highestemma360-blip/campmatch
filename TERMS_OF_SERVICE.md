# Campmatch Terms of Service

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

Welcome to Campmatch, an independent matching platform for verified university students in Ghana.

## 1. Acceptance of Terms
By registering for or using Campmatch, you agree to abide by these Terms of Service.

## 2. Eligibility
- You must be at least 18 years old.
- You must hold an active student email address from an approved university domain (`.edu.gh`).
- You must provide accurate profile information and upload genuine, clear photos of yourself.

## 3. Profile Photo Standards
All uploaded profile photos are automatically checked before publication:
- Blank, black, white, solid-color, or blurry placeholder images are not permitted.
- Every profile photo must clearly show a real person.
- Nudity, sexually explicit imagery, or inappropriate content is strictly prohibited and automatically blocked.

## 4. Acceptable Conduct
You agree not to:
- Impersonate another student or misrepresent your identity.
- Harass, threaten, stalk, or send abusive messages to other users.
- Use the platform for spam, solicitation, scraping, or unauthorized commercial activity.

## 5. Matching, Messaging & Subscriptions
- Mutual likes create a match and open private chat with a 24-hour initial response window.
- When Campmatch Plus subscriptions are active, free accounts receive a daily swipe allowance (10 swipes per 24 hours unless adjusted by administrators), while Plus subscribers receive unlimited swipes, Rewind, Beeline visibility, read receipts, and additional Super Likes.
- Subscription payments are processed via third-party gateways and are subject to provider terms.

## 6. Moderation & Account Deletion
- You may block or report any user, or delete your account at any time from Settings.
- Deleting an account immediately removes the profile from public feeds and active matches while preserving internal audit and safety records.
- Administrators reserve the right to remove violating content or suspend accounts that breach these Terms.

## 7. Disclaimer
Campmatch is an independent student platform and is not officially affiliated with UMaT or other participating universities. Users are solely responsible for their interactions both online and offline.

## 8. Contact
Questions or safety reports: `admin@st.umat.edu.gh`
